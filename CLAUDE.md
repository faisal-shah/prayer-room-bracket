# CLAUDE.md

Ceiling-mount mid-span hook for a 1 in curtain rod (see README.md for the
design basis, dimensions, printing and installation).

## Layout

- `scripts/ceiling_rod_hook.py` — the only source of geometry. Parameter block
  at the top (site measurements in mm converted from inches, then design
  choices), derived values, then build: plate → hook → fuse → stem fillet →
  counterbored holes. `result` is the bracket in design coordinates (ceiling at
  z = 0, +Y toward the room, X along the rod); `print_part` is rotated onto
  its ceiling face for slicing.
- `output/` — STEP (design coordinates), STL and 3MF (print orientation),
  regenerated by `uv run scripts/ceiling_rod_hook.py` from the repo root.
- `scripts/fea_hook.py` — FEA of the design: runs the design script with
  `--set` overrides, exports STEP, meshes with Gmsh, solves in Elmer and/or
  CalculiX, and writes `report.json` and the Elmer VTU to
  `%TEMP%\prayer-room-bracket-fea\<name>`. `scripts/fea_plots.py` draws plots
  from case directories. `scripts/fea_study.py` defines every case that
  `docs/fea.md` cites, runs them, redraws `docs/img/fea/*.png` and prints the
  numbers the report quotes. `docs/fea.md` is the analysis report.
- `docs/img/` — renders used by the README, made with the MCP `render_view`;
  `docs/img/fea/` holds the FEA plots and `failure.jpg`, cropped from the
  user's photos of the broken part.
- `.claude/skills/b123d-*` — build123d workflow skills for Claude Code.
- `.agents/skills/b123d-*` — exact mirrors for Codex; Copilot discovers and
  deduplicates both locations. Every `SKILL.md` requires `name` and
  `description` YAML frontmatter.

## Working on the geometry

- Use the build123d MCP server: `execute_file(path="scripts/ceiling_rod_hook.py", result_name="result")`,
  then `measure`, `validate`, `render_view`, `export`. `execute_file` runs in a
  clean namespace and replaces the session history, so re-`show()` anything
  you want to render afterwards.
- The script must import only from the MCP sandbox allow-list (`math`,
  `build123d`, ...). No `os`, `sys`, `pathlib`: the sandbox rejects the import
  statically even inside `if __name__ == "__main__":`. Exports use relative
  paths for that reason.
- `__name__` is `"builtins"` under `execute_file`, so the export block only
  runs standalone.
- The MCP server and standalone `uv run` environment can resolve different
  build123d versions. Avoid version-sensitive builder mutation APIs for the
  holes: build a cutter solid (`CounterBoreHole(..., mode=Mode.ADD)` in an
  empty `BuildPart`) and subtract it.
- Fillets: select edges from the same object you call `.fillet()` on
  (algebra mode). Selecting edges from `hook` and filleting inside a
  `BuildPart` that `add()`ed it fails because the builder holds a copy. A
  fillet cannot terminate where its edge chain meets a tangent edge, so the
  plate's underside perimeter is rounded as a closed loop before the fuse and
  the hook's edges are rounded on the standalone hook.
- The hook profile is built from sketch primitives because
  `EllipticalCenterArc`'s signature differs between build123d versions: the
  outer wall is the union of an `Ellipse` (stem side), the r_out `Circle`
  (open side) and a `Rectangle` joining their lowest points, which is their
  convex hull; minus the cradle `Circle`, intersected with the stem-to-lip
  wedge `Polygon`, plus the stem `Rectangle`. At `stem_t == wall_t` the
  ellipse is the circle and only it is used. `_tangent_seam` keeps the edges
  between tangent faces (stem faces into the cradle walls, the flat bottom
  into the ellipse and circle) out of the round selection. Extrude the
  profile once from x = −hook_w/2: `both=True` builds two halves whose fuse
  splits the curved faces and adds edges that the round selection then picks
  up.
- After each regeneration check: cradle inner cylinder radius 13.135 with its
  lowest point at z = −47.8, two Ø6.2 holes at (±27, 0) with Ø12 × 2.5 mm
  counterbores opening on the underside (z = −8), `clearance` against the
  reference `rod` reporting `touching`. After changing the hook or plate,
  rerun `uv run scripts/fea_hook.py --name current`; the current design's
  peak layer stress is 1.09 MPa at 4 kg (Elmer, 1.2 mm; the peak at the root
  fillet's corner shifts a few percent with the mesh and solver, so compare
  like with like). Rerun `scripts/fea_study.py` before updating `docs/fea.md`
  or its figures.

## Running the FEA

- `fea_hook.py --set` rewrites `name = value` lines in the design script's
  parameter block, which ends at the `# ---- derived ----` marker; keep that
  layout. It exec()s the design in-process, so the analysis uses exactly the
  exported geometry.
- Elmer 26.1 (`C:\Program Files\Elmer 26.1-Release\bin`) must be on `PATH`;
  executables are resolved with `shutil.which` because a bare `ElmerGrid` in
  `subprocess` failed to launch under `uv run`. This Elmer build has no MUMPS;
  CG with ILU0 is the default and the fastest (about 5 min at the default
  1.2 mm `--h-hook`).
- CalculiX is FreeCAD's bundled `ccx.exe` 2.22 (SPOOLES): 3 min at 1.4 mm,
  17 min and 9 GB at 1.0 mm. Pass it with `--ccx`. `fea_study.py` runs the
  1.0 mm case alone after the others for that reason.
- Gmsh needs the `Mesh.MeshSizeMin` floor in `build_mesh`: without it,
  curvature sizing collapses on some fillet edges and 1D meshing runs past
  8 GB.
- Stresses do not depend on E (one material, fixed supports, force loads) and
  scale linearly with `--load-kg`. Compare peak σzz with the across-layer
  strength (Prusament PLA TDS: 37.6 MPa upright).

## Decisions that are not in the code

- The 2 mm `cradle_bias` is deliberate: a hook that hangs low does not take
  weight; a slightly short one can be shimmed with washers. Keep it short.
- Screws sit on the rod's centreline either side of the hook so the load is
  between them and nothing pries; both are 87.5 mm from the wall because
  there is no room for tools between the rod and the wall.
- Stem on the wall side: hidden behind the rod, and the rod enters from the
  room side where there is space to swing it in. Tool access does not depend
  on it since the screws are above the rod.
- Layer bonding sets the strength. Printed plate-down, the stem's bending
  stress σzz crosses the layers, and an 8 mm stem (24 mm hook, R5 root
  fillet, thin shell over sparse infill) breaks along a layer just below the
  stem/cradle tangent; the FEA puts 3.7 MPa at 4 kg on both the root and that
  shoulder. The stem is 18 mm deep, grown toward the wall so the rod-side
  face, cradle and lip stay put, its outer wall tapers along an ellipse to the
  8 mm cradle wall (kept at 8 mm from under the rod to the lip), the hook is
  28 mm wide and the root fillet R8: 1.09 MPa. Deeper or wider gains a
  few percent per gram (`docs/fea.md`). Print solid.
- The plate is symmetric about the rod centreline so the hook sits centred on
  it; its 41 mm half-width is what reaches past the stem's outer face and
  fillet on the wall side. The screw holes keep 9 mm of plate from the
  counterbore to the nearest edge. Holes are counterbores for the pan-head
  #10-12 screws that come with the user's Everbilt ribbed anchors; a
  flat-seated pan head has no wedge action, which is the main way countersunk
  holes crack in printed parts.
